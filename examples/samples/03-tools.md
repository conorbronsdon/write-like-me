Every few months someone asks me which AI tool I use for writing. I use three, and I trust none of them with a first draft.

That surprises people. It shouldn't. A first draft is where you find out what you think. Hand that to a model and you get a fluent answer to a question you never quite asked. It reads well. It says nothing you'd defend in a meeting.

What I do use them for is the second hour. Cutting. Checking a number. Asking "what's the strongest objection to paragraph three?" and then arguing with the answer. That last one is worth the subscription on its own.

My rule is simple: if I couldn't have written the sentence, I don't ship it. Sometimes the model's version is better. Fine. I rewrite it until it's mine, or I delete it.

You'll find your own rule. Just make sure you have one before the tool makes it for you.
